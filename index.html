About Me

I am a Ph.D in Electrical Engineering specializing in wireless communications, artificial intelligence, and large-scale optimization. My research focuses on reinforcement learning and data-driven approaches for decision-making in complex, high-dimensional and distributed systems, with applications to next-generation (5G/6G) communication networks. I have extensive experience in designing scalable algorithms for non-convex optimization problems, particularly in user-centric cell-free massive MIMO systems. My work addresses challenges such as interference management, energy efficiency, and resource allocation under uncertainty. My research interests also extend to large language models (LLMs), generative AI, and hybrid approaches combining machine learning with classical optimization to develop robust and interpretable decision-support systems. In addition to research, I am a university lecturer and trainer in data science, Python, and software development. I am passionate about translating complex theoretical concepts into practical, real-world applications.

Research

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning, optimization, and wireless communications. I focus on developing intelligent and scalable solutions for complex systems characterized by high dimensionality, uncertainty, and dynamic environments.

Research Interests: Deep Reinforcement Learning, Large-scale optimization, Machine Learning for Energy-Efficient IoT Networks, Large language Models (LLMs) and LLM-assisted optimization, Generative AI, Learning-Based Resource Allocation in Wireless Systems, Distributed and Edge Intelligence for Communication Networks, RF/microwave, antennas
